How it works

This is a disk launcher. The little red frisbees are the disks. The blue lighting shaped bar works by pushing the disks into the (spinning if turned on) blue circle called the flywheel which will squeeze the disks against the foam bar. And then if (if it works right) it will shoot out of the end.
